# Sound effects

Every file here was trimmed, mixed to mono, normalized and re-encoded to AAC. The script that did it is
`tools/sfx-build.mjs`, and it needs the original packs downloaded.

| Files | Source | Author | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `swing*.m4a` | [20 Sword Sound Effects (Attacks and Clashes)](https://opengameart.org/content/20-sword-sound-effects-attacks-and-clashes) | StarNinjas | CC0 |
| `clash*.m4a` | same pack | StarNinjas | CC0 |
| `flesh*.m4a` (impactPunch_heavy), `plate*.m4a` (impactPlate_medium), `body*.m4a` (impactSoft_heavy) | [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 |
| `step*.m4a` (footstep00–09), `slice*.m4a` (knifeSlice) | [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) | Kenney | CC0 |
| `ring*.m4a` (metal_hit_01–05) | [100 CC0 metal and wood SFX](https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx) | rubberduck | CC0 |
| `bow1.m4a` (shoot.ogg) | [Bow & Arrow Shot](https://opengameart.org/content/bow-arrow-shot) | dorkster (mixed from qubodup's sounds) | CC-BY-SA 3.0 |
| `crowd_loop`, `murmur_loop`, `cheer_big`, `cheer_small`, `cheer_win` | [Free Crowd Cheering Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/free-crowd-cheering-sounds) | Gregor Quendel | CC-BY 4.0 |

The CC-BY and CC-BY-SA sounds need a credit in the game. The credits screen should list:
"Crowd sounds by Gregor Quendel (CC-BY 4.0) · Bow shot by dorkster, from sounds by qubodup (CC-BY-SA 3.0)".
