**Theme:** The living war — settlements now patrol, factions call the banners, and battles you can watch bleed over time instead of teleporting to a result. The player fights inside that same system.

## What shipped today

### 1. Server-side living war (`server/warfare.js`, `013_warfare.sql`)
A new server module that replaces the old instant off-map clash with three layers, all server-authoritative and offline-catch-up safe (every function runs per-tick inside `runTicks`' transaction):

- **Patrols** — every settlement musters a tier-scaled garrison ecology (a city keeps ~2 large, 5 medium, 10 small groups riding circuits around its walls; towns less; villages shelter under their town). Patrols are ordinary named warlord rows told apart by `role='patrol'` + a home leash (`home_key/home_x/home_z`, `pclass`). An enemy inside `DEFEND_R` pulls every patrol onto it. Re-muster is paced (`MUSTER_CD`, `last_muster_tick` on holds/capitals) so a wiped settlement raises replacements only so fast.
- **Campaigns** (`campaigns` table) — rarely (`CAMP.CHANCE ~1.2%/tick`, world cap 2) a strong host **calls the banners**: same-realm hosts + nearby patrols + allied-realm hosts converge on a muster point, march as one stack, and besiege an enemy hold/capital. The defender weighs the balance of power and either rides to the relief or abandons the hold. Every stage (`muster → march → siege → done`) is a first-class row so the client can draw the whole arc.
- **Timed battles** (`sbattles` table) — armies that meet **lock into a battle that bleeds over ticks** instead of resolving instantly. The map shows the bar move; walking up shows the actual lines fighting. The old instant `doClash` in `tick.js` is deleted.

### 2. The player fights inside the same system (`game.js`)
Combat is no longer its own mode — there is one combat system and it happens on the map:

- **`playerClash`** — your warband locks into the *same* map-battle machinery (`mapBattles`) the AI hosts use. The bar bleeds; you can settle it in person.
- **`dropIntoClash` / `startFieldBattle`** — drop into a clash and the two sides become one in-place **field battle where the lines stood** — the road *is* the arena (`FIELD_BATTLE_CAP 48`, the visible crowd converts 1:1, bigger hosts trickle in as reserve).
- **Sieges** — `makeGarrisonBand` / `siegeCapital` / `captureHold`: storm a hold and its garrison is a real band; take it and ownership flips (`conqueredAll` win state when every nation is yours).
- **`foldSimBattle` / `releaseFieldBand`** — sim losses fold back into the real roster: survivors carry the battle, the rest fall where the banner stood, and a surviving band returns to being map truth.
- Pre-battle **attack orders** target the nearest hostile materialised host (`nearestHostileCrowd`, `maybeOpenFieldBattleFromOrder`); the muster overlay pops in place after a win to recruit and march on.

### 3. Position-scoped world payload
- `/api/v1/world` now takes `&x=&z=` and ships **patrols near the viewer** via `tick.getArmiesNear`, plus **all hosts and anyone in a battle/campaign regardless of distance**. Old clients that send no coords get the legacy hosts-only list.
- New payload fields: `battles` (⚔ markers) and `campaigns` (muster beacons).
- The **shared world never sleeps**: live players are pure *viewers*, so `X-World: shared` requests advance the tick under them (`touchActive` = presence marker that does **not** freeze the tick). Solo worlds keep the old client-owns-motion behavior (`markActive`).

## What needs to be improved

- **Payload scoping is untuned.** `getArmiesNear` ships patrols within a radius but with 1,111 patrols alive the near-set size and wire cost are unmeasured — feed it into the perf harness's proxy metrics before M1. See [[perf-harness-and-loop]].
- **Interest management still absent.** The tick now touches ~1,300 warlord rows every beat (hosts + patrols). This is exactly the "activity buckets" headroom win the roadmap flags — patrols should tick coarsely when no player/enemy is near.
- **Touch parity.** The new battle flow (attack orders, drop-into-clash, siege muster) is keyboard/mouse only. M1's touch audit must cover it.
- **Campaign legibility on the client.** The `campaigns` rows ship (muster beacon + march + siege stages) but the client rendering of the full arc needs a pass — a muster beacon and a moving siege stack should read clearly at map zoom.
- **Co-op is parked, now doubly so.** Guest-join rode the old arena's host snapshots; with combat moved to field battles/`playerClash`, `joinCoopBattle` is explicitly parked and must be rebuilt on the new field-battle path.

## What needs to be fixed / verified

- **Not committed.** All of the above is in the working tree only. Needs a review pass + commit.
- **No golden-seed determinism test for warfare.** `tickWarfare` mutates a lot of rows per tick; the roadmap's determinism-harness rule (extend to every tick system) is unmet here. A refactor could silently fork the sim.
- **`NATIONS` / `CAP_ANGLE` / `MAP_HALF` are duplicated** between `warfare.js` and `tick.js` with "keep in sync" comments — a real drift hazard. Hoist to one shared constant.
- **Two-device shakeout still owed (M1).** Shared-world-never-sleeps changes tick ownership; needs the real multi-device test that the two hosts don't double-advance.
- **Verify patrol re-muster doesn't leak rows.** 1,111 patrols alive right now — confirm `ensurePatrols` respects quotas and doesn't grow unbounded across long offline catch-up.
